# Timeoff-demo-app — Project Plan

## What this app will do
Employees submit time-off requests for vacation, sick time, and personal holidays, and managers approve or deny them. The app keeps track of how much time off each person has earned and used, and shows everyone's approved time off on a shared yearly calendar.

## Who will use it
People at UAB only.

## What people can do with it
- Submit a time-off request by choosing the type (vacation, sick, or personal holiday), a start and end date, and the hours used each day (for example, 8 hours for a full vacation day)
- See their own requests and whether they're pending, approved, or denied
- Managers review pending requests from their team and approve or deny them
- Everyone can view a shared yearly calendar showing approved time off
- The app automatically keeps track of each person's time-off balances:
  - Vacation time is earned every month — a bit faster for people who've been here 15 years or more, a bit slower for everyone else — up to a yearly maximum. Any vacation time left over at year's end rolls into sick time instead of being lost.
  - Sick time is earned every month at the same rate for everyone, with no yearly maximum.
  - Everyone also gets a handful of personal holidays each year, which expire and reset every July 1st.

## What we'll build
A website that people open in their browser. Each person signs in with their UAB email / BlazerID before using it, and everything they submit is saved so it's there the next time they come back. Managers will see a review view for their team's pending requests, and everyone will see a yearly calendar of approved time off plus their own running balances.
